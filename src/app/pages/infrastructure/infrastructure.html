Our Infrastructure & Facilities

State-of-the-art sorting units, climate-controlled warehousing, and hygienic packaging lines.

STAGE 1

Processing & Sorting Unit

Our direct sourcing from standard farm loops is supported by advanced sorting and processing facilities. We use state-of-the-art optical color sorting machines and gravity separators to filter out impurities, stones, and off-grade grains.

This guarantees that export items like Basmati Rice, pulses, and oil seeds meet global purity specifications (admixture < 0.5%) before transport.

STAGE 2

Hygienic Packaging Facility

Hygienic packaging is crucial to prevent moisture, dust, and pest infestation during sea cargo shipping. Our facilities are certified under standard food regulations and employ semi-automated packing lines.

We offer customized packaging solutions including vacuum-sealed bags, PP/HDPE bags (from 5kg to 50kg), jute bags, and primary retail packaging branded with your logos.

STAGE 3

Warehousing & Cold Storage

We operate climate-controlled warehousing facilities with a total storage capacity exceeding 10,000 Metric Tons. Fresh harvests (like onions, potatoes, and mangoes) are routed directly into temperature-controlled cold storages to prevent spoilage.

Our dry warehouses are equipped with forced ventilation and continuous humidity monitoring systems, keeping crops clean and fresh year-round.
