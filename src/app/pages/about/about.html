About Our Company

Established leader connecting standard Indian produce and goods with overseas buyers.

OUR FOUNDATION

Over a Decade of Trust

Examine Export Services was founded with a vision to become India's most trusted channel partner for agricultural and industrial sourcing. We manage everything from farm sourcing, quality audit inspections, custom package design, container shipping, and global customs clearances.

By working closely with local farmer cooperatives and keeping strict specifications controls, we provide buyers with competitive wholesale pricing and top grading standards.

Our Mission

To deliver raw quality products directly from standard farms to international markets, ensuring customer satisfaction through transparency, fair trade, and logistics excellence.

Our Vision

To set the benchmark for global agricultural supply chains, bridging Indian produce with importing distributors worldwide with zero defect logistics.
